# Maison Goya research notes

Reviewed 16 October 2026. This is a demonstration application; product names, prices, quantities and booking schedules are illustrative.

## Brand direction

Rodrigue Vodounou describes Goya as a meeting of African roots and European experience, centered on quality, authenticity, humility and understated elegance. The founder's August 2025 interview discusses production in Italy, Benin and Senegal and the development of leather goods alongside footwear.

Source: [Forbes Afrique founder interview, 21 August 2025](https://forbesafrique.com/rodrigue-vodounou-fondateur-de-la-maison-goya/).

A February 2025 interview describes leather, raffia, Kente and Kanvo, with a balance of elegance, comfort and relaxed styling. Shoe care, polishing, patina, heel work and leather-goods restoration are meaningful parts of the brand's service identity.

Source: [Afrofeeling founder interview, 6 February 2025](https://www.afrofeeling.com/mag/article/82-la-maison-goya/).

## Product implications

- Preserve the existing dark palette, gold accents, editorial photography and serif typography.
- Put product images, fit, size availability and simple purchasing ahead of additional marketing features.
- Keep French and English equally usable, including cross-language search and product administration.
- Retain the atelier as a supporting service experience.
- Use one shared demonstration inventory. Do not imply that it represents actual boutique stock.
- Opening the installed app should lead directly to the collection; the brand story remains accessible.

## Unverified or conflicting information

The interviews document Paris, Cotonou and Dakar, and Forbes discusses a planned Abidjan opening. Present-day locations, opening hours, actual inventory and prices were not verified. The referenced brand website, goyaparis.com, was inaccessible through the research tool.

Public sources disagree about the founding year; the Forbes article also contains an internally inconsistent summary date. Do not treat the original demo's historical wording as independently verified brand copy. Confirm it with the brand before a live release.

The project's eight initial product descriptions, prices, materials and exact image-to-product assignments are demonstration content, not a verified current merchant catalog. Admin uploads and edits are available for replacing that material.
